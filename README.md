# crumbl-cookir-dashboard
This project analyzes sales performance for a cookie business using Power BI. It focuses on revenue trends, order volume, and customer behaviour across different time periods and locations.

## Data Preparation
- Cleaned raw sales dataset by removing irrelevant columns  
- Standardised customer and location information for consistency  
- Structured data to support analysis across daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly timeframes  

## Analysis Performed
- Analysed total revenue and cookies shipped over time  
- Explored sales performance across daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly periods  
- Identified trends in order volume and business performance  
- Analysed customer-related data to understand purchasing patterns  

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive Power BI dashboard for sales analysis  
- Time-based breakdown of revenue and cookies shipped  
- Customer insights linked with sales performance  
- Visual representation of business trends across multiple time periods  

## Tools Used
- Power BI  
- Excel  

## Key Insight
The analysis shows how revenue and order volume fluctuate across different time periods, helping to identify overall business performance trends and customer behaviour patterns.
